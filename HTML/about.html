About Pixelite

Technology solutions you can trust.

Our Story

Pixelite Computer Solutions was established in Cape Town in 2023 with the goal of providing affordable and reliable computer services to the local community.


What began as a small independent repair service has grown into a trusted technology support business serving students, families, gamers, remote workers and small businesses.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide affordable, reliable and professional computer repair services while delivering great customer service.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted independent computer repair businesses in the Western Cape.
